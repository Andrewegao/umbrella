Frequently asked questions

Subscriptions

What does Umbrella Pro include?

Pro unlocks the full app: route weather and trip planning, every live map layer, and your morning brief. A paid monthly or yearly subscription is required, including for everyday forecasts and widgets. A free trial may be available to eligible yearly subscribers; current prices and terms are shown in the app before purchase.

How do I cancel?

Open iOS Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, then Umbrella, then Cancel Subscription. To avoid a charge after a trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

I subscribed but the app still shows the paywall.

Tap Restore on the paywall. This asks Apple to re-check your purchases and unlocks the app if an active subscription is found. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account you subscribed with. If you have just switched phones, give it a moment and try Restore again.

My payment failed and I lost access.

Update your payment method in iOS Settings, tap your name, then Payment & Shipping. Apple will retry the charge, and your subscription resumes automatically.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase you would like refunded.

Using the app

How do I plan a trip?

Open the Trips tab, set a start and destination, and Umbrella shows the weather along the whole route plus the best time to leave to stay dry.

Location isn’t working.

Enable it in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Umbrella, and choose “While Using the App”.

I’m not getting my morning brief.

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings > Notifications > Umbrella. Umbrella stays quiet on days when nothing about the weather is worth telling you.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Apple is optional and only saves your settings for a future phone. Your subscription is tied to your Apple Account either way.

Delete your account

In the updated app, open You → Account & data, or Account & data on the subscription screen, then Delete account & synced profile. This removes your Apple sign-in details and synced name, character and unit preferences. Saved places, routes and journey history stay on this device. If you used Apple sign-in, revoke its authorization in Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Umbrella. Deleting an account does not cancel a subscription.
