Umbrella Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Umbrella (“the app”, “we”) is a weather app. We designed it to collect as little as possible. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not run any server that stores a profile of you.

What the app uses

  • Location — used to show weather for where you are and to set the starting point of a trip. Your coordinates are sent to the weather and routing services listed below in order to return a forecast or a route. They are not stored by us and are not linked to your identity.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional) — if you choose to sign in, Apple gives the app an anonymous identifier for you, and the name and email address you choose to share (Apple lets you hide your real email). This is kept on your device. Skipping sign-in does not limit any feature.
  • Your settings and character — the name you enter, your units, and your character choices are stored on your device, and mirrored to your own private iCloud storage so they survive a new phone. We cannot read your iCloud data.
  • Subscriptions — purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your card number, billing address, or Apple Account password. We only learn whether a subscription is currently active.
  • Diagnostics & usage — anonymous crash reports (Sentry) and aggregate, anonymous product-usage analytics (TelemetryDeck), used only to keep the app stable and to understand which features get used. These are not linked to your identity and are not used to track you across other apps or websites.

Services the app contacts

  • Apple WeatherKit — forecasts and weather alerts for the locations you view.
  • Our weather service — delivers our own forecast data for the locations you view.
  • OpenStreetMap routing services (FOSSGIS and Project OSRM) — when you plan a trip, the start and end coordinates of that route are sent to these services to calculate the route itself. Only the route coordinates are sent; no identifier of you accompanies them.
  • Sentry and TelemetryDeck — as described above.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or ad-tracking SDKs. The app has no ads.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not build or store a server-side profile of you — we operate no user database.

Your choices

  • Turn off location at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services.
  • Turn off notifications at any time in iOS Settings > Notifications > Umbrella.
  • Stop sharing with Sign in with Apple at any time in iOS Settings > your name > Sign in with Apple > Umbrella.
  • Delete the app to remove the data stored on your device. To also remove the mirrored copy, turn off iCloud for Umbrella in iOS Settings > your name > iCloud.

Children

Umbrella is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
