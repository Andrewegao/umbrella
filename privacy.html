Umbrella Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Umbrella is a subscription weather app. The app has no advertisements. We do not sell your personal information or use an advertising identifier (IDFA). Optional Apple sign-in details are kept on your device; we do not operate a server-side user account database.

Weather, location and routes

With your permission, the app uses your location to show forecasts and start a route. Coordinates for places and routes you choose are sent to Apple WeatherKit, our forecast service, and routing services operated by FOSSGIS and Project OSRM. These requests are needed to return weather and routes. Your saved places, routes, recent activity and cached forecasts are stored on your device. They are not sent as analytics event parameters.

Apple sign-in and iCloud

Sign in with Apple is optional. If you sign in, the app stores the Apple user identifier and any name or email address you share locally. These credentials are not sent to our own server or our analytics providers. Your chosen display name, character appearance and temperature units can be mirrored to your private iCloud key-value storage. We cannot browse your private iCloud data.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscription payments. The app checks purchase and subscription status to unlock access. We do not receive your payment-card details, billing address or Apple Account password. Subscription events and selected plans may be recorded by the analytics services below.

Usage analytics and diagnostics

Production builds use the following services to understand feature use and diagnose problems. These services may process information outside your country under their own privacy policies. Pseudonymous identifiers distinguish installations; this is not the same as collecting your name or Apple sign-in identity.

  • Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) records app interactions, onboarding steps, subscription events, app/device information and a pseudonymous app-instance identifier. It may derive approximate geography from network information. Firebase is disabled when the device region is mainland China. We use the Firebase library without advertising-ID support. The updated app also disables IDFV collection and denies advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization. We do not send your Apple sign-in details or precise coordinates to Firebase. Firebase privacy information.
  • TelemetryDeck records feature interactions and subscription events with a pseudonymous identifier and technical app/device information. We do not set an account identifier or send your name or email with these events. TelemetryDeck privacy information.
  • Sentry receives crash and app-hang diagnostics, such as stack traces, app version, device/OS information and operational breadcrumbs. Its default personally identifying information collection is disabled. Sentry privacy information.

Older versions used Firebase for aggregate acquisition and conversion measurement. The app also declares Apple SKAdNetwork identifiers for privacy-preserving installation attribution. These features measure how people discover Umbrella; they do not display ads inside the app. We do not intentionally link analytics to your identity or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and history remain until you delete them or remove the app. iCloud profile data remains until it is deleted from the app and that deletion syncs through iCloud. Analytics and diagnostics are retained according to each provider’s configuration and policies; we do not claim that uninstalling the app erases reports already sent. Aggregated or de-identified statistics may remain. Contact support for a privacy request; please do not send passwords or payment-card information.

In the updated app, open You → Account & data, or Account & data on the subscription screen, then choose Delete account & synced profile. This removes your Apple sign-in details and your local and iCloud display name, character and unit preferences. Saved places, routes and journey history remain on the device; uninstall the app to remove its local data. If you used Apple sign-in, also stop using it in Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Umbrella to revoke Apple’s authorization. Apple’s instructions.

Deleting your account or the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel your subscription with Apple.

Your controls

  • Turn off location in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.
  • Turn off notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications → Umbrella.
  • Apple sign-in is optional, and subscription purchases use your App Store account independently.

Children

Umbrella is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Support requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the contents and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot the app. Messages are handled by our email provider, Google (Gmail), and may be retained while resolving your request and for follow-up. Ask us to delete support correspondence when it is no longer needed. Do not include passwords or payment-card details.
